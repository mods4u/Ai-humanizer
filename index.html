“Human score” is a rough heuristic — it looks for AI tells like cliché phrases (delve, furthermore, em-dash overuse, drowsy sentence rhythm) versus human tells like contractions and varied pacing. Nothing is sent to a server.

How it works

🧹

Kills AI clichés

Removes tell-tale phrases like “delve into”, “in today’s fast-paced world” and “it is important to note that” — 150+ patterns and counting.

🗣️

Plain-spoken words

Swaps stiff vocabulary for everyday words: utilize → use, numerous → many, facilitate → help with.

🎵

Natural rhythm

Adds contractions, breaks up run-on sentences, trims em-dash abuse, and occasionally opens a sentence the way people actually talk.

🔒

Private by design

Everything runs in your browser with plain JavaScript. No API keys, no sign-ups, no tracking — your text never leaves your device.

Tips for best results

  • Standard intensity is a good default. Use Bold for very robotic drafts, Light when you only want gentle cleanup.
  • Hit ↻ Regenerate a few times — every pass uses a fresh random seed, so you get different word choices.
  • Always read the result once before publishing. The humanizer improves flow, but you know your meaning best.
  • Check your school or workplace policy before submitting humanized text as your own.
